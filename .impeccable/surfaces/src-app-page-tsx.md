# Surface: Home landing page (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: single-page landing demo. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: overseas importers, distributors, aquarium shops; Thai partners via TH toggle. Job: judge whether Nana Goldfish Farm can supply consistent quality at volume and ship live fish well.
Action: generic "Enquire / Contact us" (channel undecided; placeholder contact details labeled as placeholders).
Proof/content: the 7 user-supplied strengths, mission, goal. No stats, customers, countries, prices invented. Photos are labeled samples.
Constraints: red + white theme (pinned). English primary, TH/EN toggle.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The farm sells consistency, so the page is the farm's own record book: a red bookcloth ledger whose white ruled pages log, grade and chop-stamp every claim. Refuses the aquarium-photo hero + icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched red bookcloth (#C8102E family, deep #8E0B1F shadow) for cover, mission and close; white ledger paper with red double vertical rules and fine red horizontal ruling for content; ink near-black #1A1213; red chop-seal stamps (square seal, "NANA" + Thai), photo-corner pasted photographs, handwritten ink annotations. Display Chonburi (Thai+Latin), body Anuphan, hand notes Sriracha. Ledger columns, tabular numerals, no cards, no icon tiles.

STORY: Visitor opens the book, reads the farm's record of seven standards as ledger entries, sees grades as a ledger table (markets × the five judged criteria), follows one red rule tracing the batch route pond → selection → quarantine → packing → departure, reads the mission on red cloth, and opens an enquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed red bookcloth cover. Top strip: small wordmark left, TH/EN toggle and "Enquire" right. Center-left: "Nana Goldfish Farm" white-foil stamped at slab scale (~6rem; amended from red-on-red blind deboss for legibility/contrast), tagline beneath, red-on-red; white chop seal stamped lower right; a white label plate on the cover reads "Record of Thai goldfish for export" with a primary Enquire button. Right edge: the white page block peeking (page edges stack). Signature interaction: on scroll the cover swings open (3D rotateY) revealing the ruled page; entries get chop-stamped as they enter view and stay stamped.

FORM: The Farm Ledger, position 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 93116130. Raises: one continuous red route rule (neon), permanent stamps (ebru), slab headlines (blast), cover that opens (j-card).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
