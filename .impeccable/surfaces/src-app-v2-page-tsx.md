## Direction contract

THESIS: One red fish on white porcelain, the oldest luxury image of the goldfish. The page is a glazed white field that holds a single painted fish inside a porcelain plate's double red rim. Refuses the full-bleed aquarium-photo hero and the busy icon-grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool porcelain white (#FFFFFF, glaze #F4F6F5, celadon shadow #E3E9E6), ink #1C1C1C and #5E5E5E. Copper red #C21F2A only as fine double rim lines, small marks and the primary action; the logo keeps its own #EE3338. Light classical serif (Taviraj, Thai+Latin) for display, Kanit light for text. Hairline rules, gallery-matted photos (no tilt, no corners), generous asymmetric white space.

STORY: The visitor sees the farm name and one painted fish in a porcelain plate, reads the offer and the NEO-HELIOS credential, scrolls through seven standards, the grades, the route, the NEO-HELIOS appointment and the shops, then enquires.

Right 7 columns: a large porcelain plate seen from above (double red rim), the painted oranda drifting slowly inside it. Quiet top bar: logo lockup left, text links, TH/EN and Enquire right. Signature interaction: the rim lines draw in on load and the fish drifts in a slow loop within the plate; reduced motion holds it still.
